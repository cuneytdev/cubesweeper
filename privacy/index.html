1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Minesweeper: 3D Puzzle, referred to here as "the app", handles information. The app is published by TODO — LEGAL NAME, referred to here as "we" or "us".

By using the app you accept the practices described on this page. If you do not agree with them, please stop using the app.

2. Information We Collect

The app has no account system, no login and no registration. We do not ask you for your name, your email address, or any other contact detail.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress and preferences — levels completed, stars earned, the theme you selected, and your sound, haptics and quality settings — in the app's own storage on your device. This information stays on the device.

Optional gameplay statistics

The app contains an optional gameplay statistics feature. It is switched off until you agree to it, and you are asked once, on first launch. If you turn it on, the information it is designed to record is limited to: which level was played, the board size, how long the attempt took, how many lives remained, and whether the layer view was used. You can turn it off again at any time in Settings.

Verified in the current build: this feature does not transmit anything. The only implementation present writes the events to the local debug log, and the project contains no networking code of any kind. TODO — CONFIRM THIS IS STILL TRUE IN THE BUILD YOU SUBMIT. If a reporting backend is connected before release, this section and the sections on third parties, retention and international transfers must all be rewritten.

3. How Information Is Used

Information saved on your device is used to run the game: to remember where you are, to restore a game in progress, and to apply your settings.

If the optional gameplay statistics are enabled and a reporting backend is connected, the information would be used to understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to improve the game's balance. It would not be used to build a profile of you, and it would not be sold.

Conditional: the paragraph above describes an intended use. In the current build no such transmission happens. TODO — DELETE OR CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING.

4. Third-Party Services

The app is built with the Unity engine. Unity's own connected services — including Unity Analytics and Unity Ads — are disabled in this project's settings.

Apple provides the App Store and, where you have permitted it in your device settings, supplies developers with aggregated and anonymised statistics about downloads and usage. We do not control that collection. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

From source: no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK is included in the project's package list, and the only native plugin provides haptic feedback. TODO — RE-CHECK AGAINST THE FINAL BUILD, INCLUDING ANYTHING ADDED FOR RELEASE.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The app does not show advertisements. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

From source: no advertising SDK is present and the project contains no App Tracking Transparency usage string. TODO — CONFIRM, AND MAKE SURE THE APP STORE CONNECT PRIVACY ANSWERS SAY THE SAME THING.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Your progress and settings are stored on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes them.

We do not operate a server for this app, so there is no account record and no backup held by us.

Conditional: if a reporting backend is connected before release, a retention period must be stated here. TODO — STATE A RETENTION PERIOD OR CONFIRM NONE IS NEEDED.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We may disclose information if we are required to by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others — but in practice we hold no information about you that could be disclosed.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is a puzzle game with no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no purchases. It is not directed at children under 13 in particular, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

9. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security is handled by iOS and by the protections you have enabled, such as a passcode or Face ID. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Users

The app can be used anywhere the App Store operates. As the app stores data only on your device, no international transfer of your information takes place.

Conditional: this changes the moment a backend is connected, and the governing law needs naming. TODO — JURISDICTION AND, IF A BACKEND IS ADDED, TRANSFER DETAILS.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal information held about you, and to object to its processing.

Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on request. You can remove everything the app has stored by deleting the app from your device. To turn off the optional gameplay statistics, use the Settings screen inside the game.

If you believe we hold information about you and wish to exercise a right, write to SUPPORT_EMAIL.
