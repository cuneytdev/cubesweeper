Privacy Policy

Minesweeper: 3D Puzzle for iOS.

Effective date: 15 November 2026

The short version

The app has no accounts and no sign-up. It shows no advertisements and does not track you across other apps or websites. Your progress and settings are saved on your device.

The app can also send anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase — but only if you agree. It is switched off in the build, you are asked once on first launch, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings. Say no and the app sends nothing at all.

The sections below say the same thing in full.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Minesweeper: 3D Puzzle, referred to here as "the app", handles information. The app is published by Mehmet Cueneyt Dalan, referred to here as "we" or "us".

By using the app you accept the practices described on this page. If you do not agree with them, please stop using the app.

2. Information We Collect

The app has no account system, no login and no registration. We do not ask you for your name, your email address, or any other contact detail.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress and preferences — levels completed, stars earned, the theme you selected, and your sound, haptics and quality settings — in the app's own storage on your device. This information stays on the device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Optional gameplay statistics

The app includes an optional gameplay statistics setting. It is switched off until you agree to it, you are asked once on first launch, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings.

When enabled, the information it records is limited to how a level was played: which level, the board size, how long the attempt took, how many lives remained, and whether the layer view was used. It never includes your name, your contacts, your location or any device identifier.

When enabled, these events are sent to Google Firebase Analytics, and reports about crashes are sent to Firebase Crashlytics. Both ship switched off and are only ever turned on by your answer to that question; turning the setting off again stops them.

Firebase records technical information of its own alongside each event: a random identifier generated for this installation of the app, the app version, the device model, the operating system version, and the country and language the device is set to. A crash report describes the state of the app when it stopped, with the same installation identifier. None of it includes your name, your contacts, your location or the Advertising Identifier, and the identifier is replaced if you delete and reinstall the app.

3. How Information Is Used

Information saved on your device is used only to run the game: to remember where you are, to restore a game in progress, and to apply your settings.

We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell information about you.

4. Third-Party Services

The app is built with the Unity engine. Unity's connected services, including Unity Analytics and Unity Ads, are not used in this app. The app includes no advertising or attribution software.

The app uses two Google Firebase services, both behind the consent question described in section 2 and both switched off until you agree to it: Firebase Analytics for the gameplay statistics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. Google processes this information for us as our service provider. It is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by the Firebase privacy and security terms.

Apple provides the App Store and, where you have permitted it in your device settings, supplies developers with aggregated and anonymised statistics about downloads and usage. We do not control that collection and we cannot identify you from it. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The app does not show advertisements. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Your progress and settings are stored on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes them.

We operate no server of our own. There is no account, no backup and no copy of your saved game held by us.

If you allowed the optional statistics, the events and crash reports are held by Google Firebase for the retention period set on our Firebase project, and are deleted when it expires. Declining, or turning the setting off later, means there is nothing to retain.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

The only third party that receives anything is Google, as the provider of Firebase, and only for the statistics and crash reports described in sections 2 and 4.

We may disclose information if we are required to by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is a puzzle game with no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no purchases. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The optional statistics are tied to a random installation identifier rather than to a person, and they are not collected at all unless someone agrees to the question on first launch.

9. Data Security

Your saved game stays on your device, where its security is handled by iOS and by the protections you have enabled, such as a passcode or Face ID. The optional statistics travel over an encrypted connection and are held under Google's security measures for Firebase. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Users

The app can be used anywhere the App Store operates. Your saved game stays on your device. If you allowed the optional statistics, Google processes them on Firebase infrastructure that may be outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's own transfer safeguards.

This policy is governed by the laws of Türkiye.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal information held about you, and to object to its processing.

You can remove everything the app has stored by deleting the app from your device, and you can turn the optional statistics off at any time from the Settings screen inside the game, which stops any further collection.

The statistics are tied to a random installation identifier and not to your name, so we have no way to look you up. If you want the data from your installation erased, write to us from the address below and we will remove what can be identified from the information you give us.

If you believe we hold information about you and wish to exercise a right, write to minesweeper3dgame@gmail.com.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

13. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can go to minesweeper3dgame@gmail.com, or through the support page.
