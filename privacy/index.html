Privacy Policy

Minesweeper: 3D Puzzle for iOS.

Effective date: 31 October 2026

The short version

The app has no accounts and no sign-up. It shows no advertisements and does not track you. Your progress and settings are saved on your device and are not sent anywhere. We do not run a server for this app, so there is no record of you for us to hold, share or lose.

The sections below say the same thing in full.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Minesweeper: 3D Puzzle, referred to here as "the app", handles information. The app is published by Mehmet Cueneyt Dalan, referred to here as "we" or "us".

By using the app you accept the practices described on this page. If you do not agree with them, please stop using the app.

2. Information We Collect

The app has no account system, no login and no registration. We do not ask you for your name, your email address, or any other contact detail.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress and preferences — levels completed, stars earned, the theme you selected, and your sound, haptics and quality settings — in the app's own storage on your device. This information stays on the device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Optional gameplay statistics

The app includes an optional gameplay statistics setting. It is switched off until you agree to it, you are asked once on first launch, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings.

When enabled, the information it records is limited to how a level was played: which level, the board size, how long the attempt took, how many lives remained, and whether the layer view was used. It never includes your name, your contacts, your location or any device identifier.

In the current version of the app this information does not leave your device. The app contains no networking code and no analytics service that transmits anything; the setting records these events locally only. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the effective date at the top of the page will change with it.

3. How Information Is Used

Information saved on your device is used only to run the game: to remember where you are, to restore a game in progress, and to apply your settings.

We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell information about you.

4. Third-Party Services

The app is built with the Unity engine. Unity's connected services, including Unity Analytics and Unity Ads, are not used in this app. The app includes no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting software from any third party.

Apple provides the App Store and, where you have permitted it in your device settings, supplies developers with aggregated and anonymised statistics about downloads and usage. We do not control that collection and we cannot identify you from it. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The app does not show advertisements. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Your progress and settings are stored on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes them.

We do not operate a server for this app, so there is no account record, no backup and no copy of your data held by us, and therefore no retention period to state.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We may disclose information if we are required to by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others — but in practice we hold no information about you that could be disclosed.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is a puzzle game with no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no purchases. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children.

9. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security is handled by iOS and by the protections you have enabled, such as a passcode or Face ID. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Users

The app can be used anywhere the App Store operates. As the app stores data only on your device, no international transfer of your information takes place.

This policy is governed by the laws of Türkiye.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal information held about you, and to object to its processing.

Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on request. You can remove everything the app has stored by deleting the app from your device. To turn off the optional gameplay statistics, use the Settings screen inside the game.

If you believe we hold information about you and wish to exercise a right, write to cuneytdalan17@gmail.com.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

13. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can go to cuneytdalan17@gmail.com, or through the support page.
