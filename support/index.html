Minesweeper: 3D Puzzle takes the classic game off the flat grid and wraps it around a cube. Mines hide inside as well as on the surface, so solving one means thinking about every direction at once.

How to play

Six things to know. The rules are the ones you already know — the board is what changed.

1

Rotate the cube

Drag anywhere on the screen to turn the puzzle and bring a different face towards you.

2

Reveal safe cells

Tap a hidden cell to open it. If it is safe it shows how many mines sit next to it.

3

Read the clues

The number on an opened cell counts the mines touching it — in every direction, not just the four around it on one face.

4

Mark suspected mines

Press and hold a cell to put a flag on it. Hold it again to take the flag off.

5

Explore the inner layers

Use the layer controls to slice the cube open and look at the cells buried inside it.

6

Finish the puzzle

Open every safe cell. You never have to flag a mine to win — flags are there to help you keep track.

Why the numbers look bigger than you expect. On a flat board a cell touches at most 8 neighbours. Inside a cube it touches up to 26 — nine above, nine below, and eight around it. A 4 in this game is an ordinary clue, not a disaster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate the cube?

Drag across the screen to rotate the cube.

How do I reveal a cell?

Tap a hidden cell to reveal it.

How do I mark a mine?

Long-press a cell to place a flag.

How do I explore internal cells?

Use the layer controls to inspect the cube's internal layers.

What cube sizes are available?

The game supports 3×3×3, 4×4×4, 5×5×5, and 6×6×6 puzzles.

Reporting a bug

Please include your device and iOS version, the puzzle size you were playing, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. A screenshot or a short screen recording helps more than anything else.
